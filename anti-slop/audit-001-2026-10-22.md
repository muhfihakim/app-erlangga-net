## Numbered Findings & Resolution Status

### [HIGH Priority - Hard Gate]

1. **Rule R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility)**

2. **Rule R-26 (Interactive Elements & Code Hygiene)**

   - **Finding**: Dead commented-out code blocks and abandoned routes in production files.
   - **Status**: ✅ **RESOLVED**. Removed 31 lines of abandoned commented-out middleware and commented-out audio upload route.

3. **Rule R-35 (Verify Before You Deliver & Evidence)**
   - **Location**: `app/Services/GenieAcsService.php` (Line 19) & `app/Http/Controllers/SettingController.php` (Line 62)
   - **Finding**: Hardcoded fallback URL containing a specific old VPS IP address (`http://203.2.151.130:3000`).
